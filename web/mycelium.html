Mycelium · a living network that solves its environment

Thousands of hyphal tips forage across a nutrient field, lay down a permanent web, translocate sugar through it, and — by a coupled per-frame relaxation solver — thicken the routes that carry the most and prune the rest. It finds efficient supply paths. Every step is ordinary, unit-tested Rust, compiled to your GPU by rust-gpu and verifiable on the CPU. Drop food and watch it reach.
